# Recommendation deck (7 slides)

*Summer 2027 schedule recommendation, BLR hub. Synthetic practice model.*

1. **Cover.** Add, cut or re-time: where should we change the schedule?
2. **The network earns money, unevenly.** 92.5% network load factor, ₹28.2 lakh/day profit, 23 rotations, 80 passengers per direction spilled (all Mumbai).
3. **Three routes need a decision.** Table of load factor vs breakeven vs profit for all six routes (see README).
4. **Mumbai: do not add a sixth A320.** 80 spilled passengers, 44% load factor on the extra seats, -₹8.0 lakh/day. Test a fare increase first (about +₹5.6 lakh/day at +5% if demand holds).
5. **Right-size Goa and Chennai.** Goa: cut one rotation (-1.3 to +5.2 lakh/day). Chennai: ATR72 with 6 rotations (-3.1 to +6.3 lakh/day). Combined about ₹44.0 lakh/day before network effects.
6. **One timing fix.** R19 lands 10:21, missing the 10:30 bank by nine minutes. Depart 05:30, land 09:21, 69 minutes to connect. R20 and R22 need their own retime tests.
7. **Recommendation and limits.** Hold Mumbai and test pricing; cut one Goa rotation; Chennai to turboprop; retime R19. Not modelled: spill recapture, connecting revenue, fare classes, slots, price elasticity, single day.
